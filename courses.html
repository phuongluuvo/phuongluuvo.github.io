Example Course 1 – Wireless Communications

Course information

  • Code: EE301 · Level: Undergraduate · Semester: Spring 2026 · Credits: 3

  • When: Monday 09:00–11:00, Building B1, Room 201

  • Office hours: Tuesday 14:00–16:00, or by appointment

What you will learn: how a wireless link works, from path loss and fading to the capacity of a multi-user system; and how to formulate and solve a simple resource allocation problem.
