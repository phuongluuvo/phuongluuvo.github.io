Phuong Luu Vo

Associate Professor, Department of Communications Engineering, Example University
 

Welcome! I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Communications Engineering at Example University, where I lead the Wireless Networks and Optimization (WNO) Group.

My research sits at the boundary between communication theory and optimization: I work on resource allocation, stochastic and convex optimization, learning-based network control, and the performance analysis of emerging wireless systems such as reconfigurable intelligent surfaces and large antenna arrays. Please have a look at my Publications and at the work of my research group.

I am currently looking for motivated students for prethesis, thesis, and internship projects. Before you write to me, please read the Prospective Students page carefully – it explains the whole process, the requirements, and the topics that are currently open.

Research at a glance

A large part of our work can be written as a resource allocation problem. For a single-cell network with \(K\) users sharing a total power budget \(P_{\max}\), where \(p_k\) denotes the power allocated to user \(k\) and \(h_k\) its channel gain, a typical design problem is

\[ \begin{array}{ll} \mbox{maximize} & \displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{K} \log_2\!\left(1 + \frac{p_k\,h_k}{\sigma^2}\right) \\ \mbox{subject to} & \displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^{K} p_k \le P_{\max}, \qquad p_k \ge 0, \quad k = 1,\ldots,K . \end{array} \]

We are interested both in the structure of the optimal solution and in algorithms that find it with limited channel state information. See the Publications page for the papers that came out of this line of work, and this page if you want to check that the mathematics on this site renders correctly in your browser.
