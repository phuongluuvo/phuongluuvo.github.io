# news-extra.txt -- the hand-written half of the News page.
#
# News is built from two sources, newest first:
#
#   1. every entry on the Publications page -- so a paper that arrives through
#      ORCID, or that you paste into publications-extra.bib, appears in the News
#      by itself;
#   2. the lines in THIS file -- grants, talks, awards, new students, anything
#      that is not a paper.
#
# Nothing here is published; this file stays on your computer.
#
# FORMAT -- one announcement per line:
#
#     YYYY-MM-DD | the text of the announcement
#
# The date must come first, then a vertical bar, then the text. Write the month
# as two digits. Items are sorted automatically, so the order of the lines does
# not matter. Anything after the date can be plain text, and
# [a label](https://link) becomes a link.
#
# EXAMPLES -- uncomment (delete the leading "#") to use:
#
# 2026-09-01 | Three new papers accepted at [IEEE ICC 2026](https://icc2026.ieee-icc.org/).
# 2026-06-15 | Invited talk at the Vietnam School of Information Theory.
# 2026-03-02 | Welcome to two new thesis students joining the group this term.
# 2025-11-20 | Awarded a two-year research grant by the national foundation.
#
# Only the two most recent years are shown in full on the News page; older years
# are collapsed behind a click. The Home page always shows the four newest items.
#
# After editing, run:  make update
