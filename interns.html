Interns

Short research internships in the group

We host a small number of interns every year. Internships are usually short projects (8–16 weeks) that can be done alongside coursework, and they are the most common way to join the group later as an honors or Master student.

What an internship looks like

  • Duration: 8–16 weeks, 8–12 hours per week.

  • Starting point: a well-defined sub-problem with a concrete deliverable (a simulation, a reproducible experiment, or a short technical note).

  • What you get: weekly supervision, access to our simulation code, and a reference letter if the work goes well.

  • How to apply: see the Prospective Students page.
