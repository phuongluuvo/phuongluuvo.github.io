Biography

Career, research interests, and background

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Communications Engineering at Example University. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering and have since worked on the mathematical foundations and the practical algorithms behind modern wireless networks.

My work combines tools from convex and stochastic optimization, queueing and network economics, and – more recently – machine learning. The common thread is that wireless systems are shared resources, so almost every interesting question is an optimization question.

Research interests

  • Resource allocation and scheduling in wireless networks

  • Convex, stochastic, and distributed optimization

  • Learning-based control and reinforcement learning for networks

  • Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces and large-scale antenna systems

  • Performance analysis, energy efficiency, and network economics
